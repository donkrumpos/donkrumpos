All long as I have known Don, he's been a leader in the arts. His distinctive personal voice comes through in every design and printmaking project he's involved with. He is deeply invested in the proliferation of the arts through academics and by meeting people in the streets through curated shows at his gallery, the Jabberwock in Algoma, Wisconsin. Due to his extensive expertise and ability to connect with people, he has been asked to teach at various universities throughout the region. Don's work as an educator and gallerist has played a vital role in refining the Fine Arts culture throughout the Northeastern Wisconsin region.
