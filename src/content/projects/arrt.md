The American Registry of Radiologic Technologists needed a microsite to gather nominations for the best in its field. I created the brand for it, *I Am the Gold Standard*, and a pleasant experience for nominating someone.

The next year ARRT wanted attention at a trade show. I came up with a quiz for its members to play, full of inside jokes from the profession.
