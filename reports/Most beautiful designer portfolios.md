# Ten portfolios worth stealing from, ranked

The most beautiful individual designer portfolios online in late 2026 are not the award-winning WebGL showpieces. They are a small set of sites built on one strong idea each. Gianluca Gradogna's cinematic split frame, Rauno Freiberg's framed and dated craft tiles, and Shirley Wu's gallery-quiet studio page lead the eyeball ranking, which came from rendering every candidate at 1440px on 2026-11-10. The editorial type-led sites (Santa Maria, Appleton, Mod, Ango) and the hand-led sites (Hische, Wu) are closest to donkrumpos.com's field-journal system. The list's one true case-study site (Valdivia) shows how hard it is to find public product work presented beautifully. Three gaps run through all ten, and donkrumpos could own all three. None captions its images. None uses a printmaking hand. None puts a map at the centre of an editorial site. Two caveats apply. The browser rendered in dark mode, so Santa Maria, Appleton and Ango were judged on their dark themes. And "beautiful" here is one careful viewer's verdict, not a jury score.

## The ranked ten

The order follows the rendered visual check. It covers four kinds of site: editorial and type-led (4, 6, 7, 9, 10), crafted design-engineer (2, 4), case-study model (8), and hand- or art-led (1, 3, 5).

### 1. Gianluca Gradogna — [gianlucagradogna.com](https://gianlucagradogna.com/)

**Who:** a Florence-based designer working across design, advertising, code and photography. He won Awwwards Portfolio Honors and Site of the Day in January 2025 ([Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/gianluca-gradogna-portfolio)).

**Why it's beautiful:** a split screen. On the left, a white card reading "Crafting Narrative Through Design." sits over a black-and-white portrait. On the right, a full-bleed jungle photograph carries a serif "THROUGH THIS LENS" and an inset photo. The type pairs a serif ("LZ") with PP Neue Montreal. It was the most cinematic site in the set.

**Weakness:** the content sits behind a WebGL preloader ("Loading portfolio", counter at 0). That hurts first paint and leaves crawlers with nothing to read.

**Borrow:** the clearest model for **Two Hands**. Use one frame with two registers, a quiet type card on one side and a full-bleed image world on the other. The same framing also works for "the Lens". Leave out the preloader.

### 2. Rauno Freiberg — [rauno.me](https://rauno.me/)

**Who:** a Staff Design Engineer at Vercel who previously worked at The Browser Company. He made Devouring Details and the Web Interface Guidelines. The site won an Awwwards Honorable Mention in 2025 ([Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/portfolio-2025)).

**Why it's beautiful:** the homepage sets his name as an oversized grotesk sentence on a white card with a huge yellow circle. The [/craft](https://rauno.me/craft) page is a dated grid of about 30 studies. Blurred previews sharpen as they load, each tile carries a "View Production" or "Read Essay" label, and the card frames never vary. Behind the scenes, **65 muted looping videos and 83 reserved aspect-ratio boxes** mean the layout never shifts. It was the most "crafted" site in the set. His nav even has a "Field Notes" section.

**Weakness:** it reads as a design-engineer reel. There are no problems, decisions or outcomes, and no client framing.

**Borrow:** treat a consistent framed tile with a date and a label as an image system. That is what the umber plates should become. Reserve every plate's aspect ratio, and use muted, looping `playsinline` clips rather than GIFs for map interactions.

### 3. Shirley Wu — [shirleywu.studio](https://shirleywu.studio/)

**Who:** an artist working at the meeting point of art and data. Her tagline is "Art, data, and the creative process" ([shirleywu.studio](https://shirleywu.studio/)).

**Why it's beautiful:** the page has a script monogram, a tiny italic nav (art / notebook / about), and one huge exhibition photo of her framed, hand-drawn data piece on a gallery wall. Type is GT Alpina with GT Alpina Typewriter, so one family covers both serif and mono. It came closest to the feeling of "handmade work, shown with restraint".

**Weakness:** extremely sparse. There is no bio and nothing explains what she does for clients.

**Borrow:** photograph the murals and woodcuts *as installed objects*, in place and at scale, rather than as flat crops. The single-image confidence is worth copying too. Her one-family serif-plus-typewriter system is also a useful check on whether Fraunces + Newsreader + Plex Mono is one voice too many.

### 4. Antoine Wodniack — [wodniack.dev](https://wodniack.dev/)

**Who:** a French creative developer. He won Awwwards Portfolio Honors and Site of the Day in late 2024, and the site is still current ([Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/aw-portfolio)).

**Why it's beautiful:** it is *not* the restrained editorial site the text-only notes described. It is loud brutalism: full-bleed hot red, a black condensed "CREATIVE · DEVELOPER", an animated line-wave field, binary ticker strips, and a boxed grid header with a QR code. Its control and conviction are total. It runs on **Astro + Cloudflare**, the same stack as donkrumpos. Fonts are PP Editorial New, PP Fraktion Mono and Bigger Display.

**Weakness:** close to spectacle, and not a style match. The 34 numbered project cards just link out to client sites, with no case studies.

**Borrow:** the commitment, not the colour. Pick one colour and use it at full strength (the umber band could carry this). Use a boxed grid header and numbered catalogue codes on the work.

### 5. Jessica Hische — [jessicahische.is](https://jessicahische.is/)

**Who:** a letterer, illustrator and author.

**Why it's beautiful:** the best example in the set of an artist's own hand serving as the brand. Her lettering logo is self-hosted as a font called `'Jess Hische Font No Stealing Plz'`. It sits over Mercury, a sober book serif, with a grid of lettering thumbnails, italic "Most Recent" labels and small-caps categories on near-white cream (`#fafaf8`) ([jessicahische.is](https://jessicahische.is/)).

**Weakness:** a newsletter modal pops up on load, which is a bad first touch. The pink palette is personal and doesn't transfer.

**Borrow:** this is the direct analogue for the woodcut hand. Let the hand be the display layer only (masthead, section marks, plate numbers) and keep Newsreader doing the reading. Never open with a modal.

### 6. Jason Santa Maria — [jasonsantamaria.com](https://jasonsantamaria.com/)

**Who:** a designer with more than 25 years of experience. He was formerly at Shopify, Netlify and *Slate*, is Creative Director of A Book Apart, wrote *On Web Typography*, and now consults through Loud Room for non-profits, foundries and publishers ([jasonsantamaria.com](https://jasonsantamaria.com/)).

**Why it's beautiful:** a big serif lede ("is a designer with over 25 years…") sits next to a round portrait and an inset dark panel. It is calm, confident and typographic. Under the hood is a serif / sans / mono trio (Roslindale, HEX Franklin, VCTR Mono), the same structure as Fraunces / Newsreader / Plex Mono. The page uses asterisk rules, credits its typefaces in the footer, and keeps an archive of five past versions.

**Weakness:** a résumé in good type. There is no work imagery at all.

**Borrow:** use it as the model for the About page and the homepage intro sentence. Add a **colophon** that credits the faces, which is a cheap book convention that signals craft, and a "past versions" archive.

### 7. Maggie Appleton — [maggieappleton.com](https://maggieappleton.com/)

**Who:** a self-described "designer, anthropologist, and mediocre developer" who makes visual essays and works at GitHub Next ([maggieappleton.com](https://maggieappleton.com/)).

**Why it's beautiful:** a large serif headline, "Maggie makes visual essays about…", over a small sans subline and a colourful monogram. It is warm and legible. Canela Deck and Canela Text sit on cream (`#f6f5f1`). Her dark mode is a *warm* near-black (`#1c1b18`), not a cold grey, which is directly relevant to dark umber plates.

**Weakness:** dates show only as relative phrases ("about 1 year ago"). Lato is a generic UI partner for Canela, and the card-grid homepage is less editorial than her essays.

**Borrow:** the "[Name] makes [thing] about [subject]" intro sentence, and a warm-dark token set for the plates and any dark mode.

### 8. Gabriel Valdivia — [gabrielvaldivia.com](https://www.gabrielvaldivia.com/work)

**Who:** an independent product design and strategy consultant. His past work includes Facebook 360 and Patreon 2.0.

**Why it's beautiful:** a black page with a big grotesk line, "Fractional Head of Design for early-stage teams", which states his positioning in one sentence. The work index has **sector chip filters** (Healthcare, Enterprise, Civic Tech, AI…). Each case opens with a credits block (Team, Services, Date), then sections that pair each design decision with its rationale ([Daylight case](https://www.gabrielvaldivia.com/work/daylight)). It was the strongest live full-case-study site the research found.

**Weakness:** the rendered thumbnails are **glossy 3D device mockups**, which breaks donkrumpos's "no device mockups" rule. The cases also have no outcomes or captions, and no link to the next case.

**Borrow:** the one-line positioning, the sector chips (so Optum and NielsenIQ can sit beside murals and maps without clashing), and the credits block. Leave the imagery.

### 9. Craig Mod — [craigmod.com](https://craigmod.com/)

**Who:** a writer, photographer and photo-book maker who writes about walking and Japan. His *Things Become Other Things* (Random House, 2025) appeared on several best-of-year lists, according to his site ([craigmod.com](https://craigmod.com/)).

**Why it's beautiful:** the beauty is in the individual essay and book pages, not the homepage. They use book-designer type (FF Meta Serif with small caps), "* * *" dividers, and a warm, self-deprecating voice.

**Weakness:** the homepage leads with commerce. A newsletter signup form, laurel badges and a book promo fill the area above the fold.

**Borrow:** a *voice* reference rather than a visual one. He is the clearest model for tying place, walking and printed objects into a practice, which is exactly Place & Story. Field Notes should read like his essay pages.

### 10. Steph Ango — [stephango.com](https://stephango.com/)

**Who:** a designer behind Obsidian's Minimal theme, with a background in inks and dyes (Inkodye, Lumi).

**Why it's beautiful:** a dark, minimal list of writing in dated rows ("2025 · 10"). The real artefact is **[Flexoki](https://stephango.com/flexoki)**, a published palette "inspired by analog inks and warm shades of paper" (paper `#FFFCF0`), which he built for this site.

**Weakness:** visually plain otherwise, with system-default typography.

**Borrow:** the dated mono ledger is exactly the "Also" ledger the direction doc proposes. Publishing the field-journal palette and type spec as its own page would show systems thinking for a "builder" in the same way Flexoki does.

## What this means for donkrumpos

- **Captioned plates are open territory.** The case-study research found **zero `<figcaption>` elements** on Valdivia's, Kosas's or Rauno's craft pages. A dark umber plate with a numbered mono caption (plate number, what to look at, the decision it shows), a reserved aspect ratio and a looping clip would combine Rauno's tile discipline with something no one on this list does.
- **Lead with one serif sentence.** Santa Maria, Appleton and Valdivia all win the first second with a single large sentence that says who they are. Two Hands should open the same way, laid out as Gradogna's two-register split.
- **The hand works best as the display layer.** Hische's custom lettering over Mercury is the proof. Keep the woodcut to mastheads, marks and plate numbers, and let Newsreader carry the reading. Among the sites and juried winners checked, **no recent portfolio uses a printmaking hand**, so this is distinctive rather than derivative.
- **Check the paper tone.** The elite warm sites keep their paper close to white (`#f6f5f1`, `#fafaf8`, `#FFFCF0`). Confirm that the cream reads as "fine paper" rather than "parchment". Appleton's warm near-black is the reference for the plates.
- **Avoid their first-touch mistakes.** Three of the ten fumble the first touch: Hische's modal, Gradogna's preloader and Mod's commerce-led top. The Lens already has a poster for first paint, so keep that discipline. No researcher found a personal site that pairs a map homepage with editorial type at this level.

## Honorable mentions and dropped

**Dropped after the visual check:**
- **paco.me** has a single italic-serif line on a dark page (it shares Newsreader with donkrumpos). It is too empty to serve as a portfolio model.
- **johnyvino.com** has a dot-matrix "Humanize Design" headline on black, and none of his enterprise work shows above the fold.

**Honorable mentions (not rendered, so treat as leads):**
- **[jakub.kr](https://jakub.kr/work/drag-gesture)** embeds live DOM specimens in place of screenshots. Its pattern fits a small live MapLibre plate in the map-tools case.
- **[mattstromawn.com](https://mattstromawn.com/)** uses a Charter / Inter / Geist Mono trio for a designer-leader.
- **[macwright.com](https://macwright.com/)** shows engineering and cartography taste in a single family, Century Supra.
- **[andywoodruff.com](https://andywoodruff.com/)** has beautiful maps in a generic Lato shell, which shows the cartography gap.
- **[elliotjaystocks.com](https://elliotjaystocks.com/)** is typography-led but only lightly verified.
- **[karoliskosas.com](https://karoliskosas.com/cinemaclub/)** has real "My role / Results" case-study structure.

**Spectacle benchmarks, deliberately excluded:** Bruno Simon (Awwwards Site of the Month, January 2026, and CSSDA Best Portfolio 2025), Jesper Landberg and Pacôme Pertant. All three are three.js showpieces ([CSSDA 2025](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/blog/2025-website-of-the-year-winners/430/)).

**Stale or gone:**
- Simon Pan's once-canonical Uber case study is now password-gated (401).
- Eleanor Lutz's Tabletop Whale has been dormant since 2020. It is still worth studying for maps presented as specimen plates.
- The awarded bleibtgleich.com portfolio has been replaced by an unrelated site.
- Taylor Roy's Fonts In Use "Fraunces" entry no longer matches her live site, which now uses only Inter.
- Typewolf's "2026" portfolio list is made up of features from 2015–2020 ([Typewolf](https://typewolf.com/portfolio-sites)).
