# Most admired individual designer portfolios: curated galleries and design press, 2025–2026

Research date: 2026-11-10. I checked every URL below with a live HTTP fetch on 2026-11-10, and all returned 200. I took font and color evidence from each site's HTML and CSS, using curl and grep. I did not render any site visually, so I can't vouch for motion, image framing or layout beyond what the press or the page text says.

## Q1: Which individual portfolios recur across curated galleries and "best portfolios 2025/2026" roundups?

### Takeaway
The galleries hardly overlap. Muzli's 2026 list of 100, SiteInspire's recent feed and Codrops' 2026 portfolio case studies each surface a different set of mostly motion-heavy, WebGL or Framer/Webflow sites, made by art directors, photographers and creative developers. No single individual site showed up in more than one gallery I checked. "Recurrence" exists only within a gallery's own feed. Across galleries, only reputation repeats, for a small canon of design-engineer sites (see Q2).

### Cited Findings
- **Muzli, "100 Best Designer Portfolio Websites of 2026"** (Eyal Zuri, published 2025-10-12; the order is explicitly random). Individual entries include Miłosz Chudy / bychudy (art director, graphic designer, photographer; album and poster art, Spotify collaborations), Clay Boan (multidisciplinary designer, NYC; Nike, Apple and Google), Karim Saab (art director and front-end developer; Webflow, GSAP, WebGL, Three.js), Nathan Leigh Davis / Fine Thought (web engineer, "minimalist experimental"), Clément Grellier (front-end developer, "micro-interactions and minimal aesthetics") and Marga Navarro (product designer, Webflow prototypes) — [Muzli](https://muz.li/blog/top-100-most-creative-and-unique-portfolio-websites-of-2025/)
- **SiteInspire's recent portfolio-category features (2026)** include Olivia Plender (2026-11-10), David McGillivray / dmcg.co (2026-10-16), Sam Thies (2026-09-30), Alicia Dubuis (2026-09-30), Félix Péault (2026-09-28), Artemii Lebedev (2026-09-23), Maximilian Kaspar (2026-09-22), Kai Blamey Nguyen (2026-09-21) and Emi Takahashi (2026-09-11) — [SiteInspire](https://www.siteinspire.com/websites?categories=portfolio)
- **Codrops, 2026-03-05**: a case study of Corentin Bernadou's portfolio (freelance developer), headlined "Swiss-Inspired Layouts, WebGL Geometry, and Thoughtful Motion". A companion piece covers its shader structure and animation choreography. Abduzeedo also covered it ("WebGL Portfolio Blends Swiss Design and Code") — [Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/03/05/inside-corentin-bernadous-portfolio-swiss-inspired-layouts-webgl-geometry-and-thoughtful-motion/), [Abduzeedo](https://abduzeedo.com/node/89093). Codrops returned a 403 to my fetch, so these details come from search snippets.
- **Other Codrops 2026 portfolio pieces** (from search listings, not read in full): Artem Shcherban, "Designing Against the Gallery" (2026-05-02); Jackie Zhang, a portfolio "that actually feels like them" (2026-03-20); HAOQI.DESIGN, a retro-futurist portfolio with glass shaders (2026-08); and Alex Tkachev, a designer who made his portfolio "almost invisible" (2026-10-02) — [Codrops search listing](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/10/02/alex-tkachev-designing-less/)
- **Typewolf's "Favorite Portfolio Sites" list** is titled 2026, but its entries are largely older Site of the Day picks (several slugs end in -2019). It is useful for serif-led examples: Thibaud Allie (Roslindale), Aristide Benoist (Schnyder), Jennifer Heintz (Editorial New), Matthew Galloway (GT Sectra), Lisa Nettler (Lyon Text), Joy Li (IM Fell French Canon) and Érika Moreira (Poynter Oldstyle) — [Typewolf](https://www.typewolf.com/portfolio-sites)
- **Godly** now presents itself as "Recent (previously Godly)", per a third-party directory. I found no individual-portfolio listing from it — [UIUX Showcase](https://uiuxshowcase.com/resources/godly-website/)

### Inferences
- The galleries reward spectacle (WebGL, kinetic type, full-bleed photography). The design-engineer canon rewards restraint and craft. An editorial "field journal" site sits between the two and is closer in spirit to the restraint camp.
- Serif- and print-led individual portfolios are scarce in the 2025–26 gallery feeds. Typewolf's older list is the best catalog of them, but its entries are not current.

### Gaps
- I couldn't access Godly/Recent, Minimal Gallery, Land-book, Httpster, One Page Love, Bestfolios or Cosmos feeds in the time budget. I can't say what they feature in 2025–26.
- I found no It's Nice That, Eye on Design/AIGA, Creative Bloq, Figma blog or Framer/Webflow showcase roundup from 2025–26 that names individual portfolios. The Creative Bloq "10 famous web designer portfolios" piece surfaced but is undated and unread.
- Read.cv shut down. I didn't identify or check a successor's featured profiles.

## Q2: Which are cited by designers as reference-quality, and the shortlist?

### Takeaway
The durable canon of reference-quality personal sites belongs to product designers and design engineers: Rauno Freiberg, Paco Coursey, Emil Kowalski, Brian Lovin and Rasmus Andersson. Writer-designers like Frank Chimero and Craig Mod add the editorial and print tradition. Combined with the 2026 gallery picks, they give the shortlist below. The press support is thin: the design-engineer canon is documented mainly through curated personal lists and interviews, not formal 2025–26 roundups.

### Cited Findings
- **rauno.me** gets the most citations in curated personal-site collections, for example a Raindrop "Personal Websites" collection and a dev.to roundup. The roundup praises its "shader effects, how it fades away to reveal the content when you scroll", its macOS-dock-like navigation and its interactive Craft demos — [dev.to (2023)](https://dev.to/felixrunquist/web-design-inspiration-ten-perfect-examples-1k0l), [Raindrop](https://raindrop.io/danielwirtzx/personal-websites-16881384). Rauno is a Staff Design Engineer at Vercel, ex-The Browser Company — [Lovers Magazine interview](https://spaces.is/loversmagazine/interviews/rauno-freiberg)
- **brianlovin.com** appears in a public list of designer personal sites tagged #designer — [histre notes](https://histre.com/public/notes/reorx/tag/designer)
- I found no search-surfaced citations for paco.me or emilkowal.ski. Their standing comes from reputation in the design-engineering community, which I could not source in this pass.

### Shortlist (all confirmed live, HTTP 200, 2026-11-10)

**Editorial / print / crafted sensibility (closest fit for donkrumpos.com)**

1. **Paco Coursey — https://paco.me/** — design engineer (Webmaster at Linear; previously the Vercel design system, website and dashboard; author of cmdk and next-themes).
   - *Featured:* reputation and canon only. No 2025–26 gallery feature found.
   - *What's beautiful:* type is Newsreader alongside Söhne and Inter (the webfont files are newsreader-subset, sohne-subset and inter-subset), so it shares **Newsreader** with donkrumpos.com. Single text column. Section heads with hairline rules (Building / Projects / Writing / Now / Connect). Spare, poetic copy that closes on "Pray at the altar of hard work." The footer reads 2025.
   - *Weaknesses:* almost no imagery or case-study depth. It's a link index, not a portfolio, and assumes the reader already knows his work.
   - [Live fetch](https://paco.me/)
2. **Craig Mod — https://craigmod.com/** — writer, photographer and book designer.
   - *Featured:* reputation only. No 2025–26 citation found.
   - *What's beautiful:* a book-designer's site. It is set in FF Meta (ff-meta-web-pro), with "* * *" section dividers, prominent book covers, fine printed-in-Japan editions, walking newsletters (*Ridgeline*, *Roden*) and a warm, self-deprecating voice ("thanks for visiting my little nook"). The footer reads "Whacked together by Craig Mod since 2002." It is the strongest analog to a **place-and-story practice**: walks, place and printed objects.
   - *Weaknesses:* very long, commerce-heavy home page with repeated subscribe and buy CTAs. The page gives two different subscriber counts (~50,000 and ~40,000+).
   - [Live fetch](https://craigmod.com/)
3. **Frank Chimero — https://frankchimero.com/** — designer and writer (*The Shape of Design*), NYC.
   - *Featured:* reputation only. No 2025–26 citation found.
   - *What's beautiful:* radical restraint. The home page is a name, "New York-based designer", and five dated links (Studio Frank; The Shape of Design Book · 2012; Dust Collages · 2017–2020; About; Post Archive 2009–2025). The CSS references a Courier/mono stack. It reads as a table of contents.
   - *Weaknesses:* the home page shows no work at all, and I couldn't verify its visual design.
   - [Live fetch](https://frankchimero.com/)
4. **Rauno Freiberg — https://rauno.me/** — interaction designer and design engineer (Vercel; Devouring Details).
   - *Featured:* recurring in curated lists (see above).
   - *What's beautiful:* shader scroll-reveal, dock-style nav, an interactive **Craft** gallery of micro-interaction demos, and a terse manifesto ("Make it fast. Make it beautiful. Make it consistent. … Make it."). Background is #FAFAFA. Its nav includes a **"Field Notes"** section, the same name as the donkrumpos.com notes collection.
   - *Weaknesses:* craft-for-craft's-sake. Little client and outcome framing, and the experience depends on JavaScript.
   - [Live fetch](https://rauno.me/)
5. **Brian Lovin — https://brianlovin.com/** — product designer.
   - *Featured:* designer-tagged personal-site lists (histre).
   - *What's beautiful:* the CSS pairs **Source Serif 4** with Inter and a mono, which is a serif-led product-designer site with a structure close to donkrumpos.com's three-family system.
   - *Weaknesses:* I didn't inspect its layout or content in depth.
   - [Live curl](https://brianlovin.com/)

### Inferences
- The best fit for the field-journal direction comes from writer-designers and design engineers (Paco, Mod, Chimero, Lovin, Rauno), not from gallery picks. They win on voice, typographic restraint and a sense of a person behind the work.
- Paco (Newsreader) and Lovin (Source Serif 4) show that serif-led text in a product-designer context reads as "crafted", not "old".
- Mod is the clearest model for linking place, walking and printed matter to a practice, which fits the Place & Story positioning.
- None of the shortlist uses a woodcut or printmaking hand. That territory looks open among product-designer portfolios.

### Gaps
- I didn't confirm any 2025–26 press feature (It's Nice That, AIGA Eye on Design, Creative Bloq, Figma) for any shortlist site.
- I found no X, Bluesky or Are.na citations directly. The Are.na portfolio channels weren't searched.
- I didn't do visual inspection or screenshots, so motion, grid and image-framing descriptions are limited to press headlines and the CSS evidence above.
- Codrops pages returned 403, so the Bernadou details rely on search snippets.
