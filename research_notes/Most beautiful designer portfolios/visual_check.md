# Visual check (coordinator, Claude in Chrome / Brave, 2026-11-10)

The researchers fetched HTML only. These are verdicts from actually rendering each site at 1440px.
Caveat: the browser was in dark color-scheme, so sites with a dark mode rendered dark
(Santa Maria, Maggie Appleton, Steph Ango, paco.me). Light-mode looks were not seen.

These eyeball findings OVERRIDE text-only descriptions in the other notes where they conflict.

- **wodniack.dev** — NOT restrained/editorial. Loud brutalist: full-bleed hot red (#ff0a45-ish), black
  condensed display "CREATIVE · DEVELOPER", animated line-wave field, binary ticker strips, boxed grid
  header with QR code. Serif only in small header text. Huge control and confidence; spectacle-adjacent.
  Lesson: a single committed color + grid-box header + total conviction. Not a stylistic match.
- **craigmod.com** — top of homepage is a newsletter signup form, laurel badges, then book promo. Busy,
  commerce-led above the fold. Beauty lives in individual essays/books pages, not the homepage. Keep as
  a Place & Story *voice* reference, not a visual one.
- **jasonsantamaria.com** — big serif lede ("is a designer with over 25 years…") next to a round
  portrait, inset dark panel. Calm, confident, typographic. Good model for the About / homepage intro.
- **maggieappleton.com** — large serif headline "Maggie makes visual essays about…", small sans
  subline, colorful monogram. Warm and legible. Strong intro-sentence pattern.
- **rauno.me** — oversized grotesk name-as-sentence on a white card with a huge yellow circle;
  horizontally scrolling cards. /craft: dated grid of tiles with blurred previews resolving on load,
  "View Production / Read Essay" labels, consistent card frames. The most "crafted" feel of the set.
  Lesson: consistent framed tiles with date + label = an image system.
- **gabrielvaldivia.com** — black page, big grotesk "Fractional Head of Design for early-stage teams"
  (clear positioning in one line). Work index has a sector chip filter (Healthcare, Enterprise,
  Civic Tech…). BUT the thumbnails are glossy 3D device mockups (tablet, phone+laptop). Contradicts
  donkrumpos's "no device mockups" rule — borrow the chips and positioning line, not the imagery.
- **jessicahische.is** — her lettering logo, centered intro, grid of lettering thumbnails with italic
  "Most Recent" labels + small caps categories. A newsletter modal pops on load (bad first touch).
  Best example of the artist's own hand as the brand.
- **paco.me** — almost nothing: name, one italic-serif + sans line, dark page. Pure restraint; too
  empty to be a portfolio model. Drop from top 10.
- **shirleywu.studio** — script monogram, tiny italic nav (art / notebook / about), one huge
  exhibition photo of her framed hand-drawn data piece on a gallery wall. Gallery-like, quiet, elite.
  Closest *feeling* to "hand-made work shown with restraint."
- **johnyvino.com** — black page, dot-matrix "Humanize Design" headline. Dark, techy; nothing of the
  enterprise work visible above the fold. Weak first impression. Drop.
- **gianlucagradogna.com** — split screen: white card "Crafting Narrative Through Design." over a
  B&W portrait; right half a full-bleed jungle photo with serif "THROUGH THIS LENS" and an inset photo.
  Genuinely beautiful, cinematic. Relevant to Two Hands (split frame, two registers).
- **stephango.com** — dark, minimal list of writing with dated rows "2025 · 10". The dated mono ledger is
  exactly the "Also" ledger the direction doc proposes. Visually plain otherwise.

Quick ranking by eyeball beauty: Gradogna, Rauno, Shirley Wu, Wodniack, Hische, Santa Maria, Appleton,
Valdivia, Mod (essay pages), Ango. Dropped: paco.me, johnyvino.com.
