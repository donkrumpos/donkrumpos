# Award-recognized individual designer / designer-developer portfolios, 2025–2026

Research date: 2026-11-10. Method: Awwwards category listings (Portfolio Honors, Typography Honors), individual Awwwards site pages (tags, descriptions, dates), CSS Design Awards WOTY 2025 results, plus a live fetch (HTTP status, page title, font files and JS libraries in the HTML source) of every candidate URL.

**Main limitation:** I could fetch HTML and text but could not render pages or see screenshots. The visual descriptions below come from three things: (a) the jury tags and descriptions on Awwwards, (b) fonts and libraries found in the page source, and (c) page text and structure. Anything beyond that is marked as inference. Before anyone cites a site as "beautiful", a person should look at it in a browser.

## Which individual (not agency/studio) portfolio sites won Awwwards SOTD/SOTM or Portfolio-category honors in 2025–2026?

### Takeaway
The Awwwards Portfolio Honors list for Jan 2025 to Sep 2026 has about 11 individual practitioners among roughly 20 winners. The rest are studios and agencies. Bruno Simon's 3D portfolio is the most decorated: Awwwards SOTM Jan 2026 and CSSDA Best Portfolio 2025. Most individual winners are French or European "creative developers" working in GSAP/WebGL. The jury-recognized pool is weighted heavily toward motion and 3D.

### Inferences

## Which of these show restraint and editorial/typographic control rather than pure WebGL spectacle?

### Takeaway
The restrained, type-led end of the award pool is Antoine Wodniack, Gianluca Gradogna, Olha Lazarieva, Rauno Freiberg, Grégory Lallé, Mat Voyce and Valentina Pastushenko. Wodniack and Gradogna are the only ones with a confirmed display serif, which makes them closest to donkrumpos's serif-led field-journal system. The rest are motion or 3D spectacles.

### Cited Findings
- **Antoine Wodniack (wodniack.dev).** Self-hosted fonts: PP Editorial New (Regular + Ultralight, a narrow display serif), PP Fraktion Mono and Bigger Display. Built with Astro on Cloudflare (`/_astro/` and `/cdn-cgi/` paths), the same stack as donkrumpos.com. The page is one long editorial scroll: header with a "Change contrast" toggle; hero "Coding globally from France. Available for freelance work"; heading "Creative ★ Developer"; About with career milestones; an Awards list; 34 numbered project cards (codes like "#3vva-0000/34"); a captioned gallery of personal history; footer "Coding my way since 1987". Binary-digit rows act as section dividers. Awwwards tags: minimal, retro, typography, experimental. Weakness: the work cards are only names and links to client sites, with no case studies, outcomes or roles. — [wodniack.dev (fetched)](https://wodniack.dev/); [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/aw-portfolio)
- **Gianluca Gradogna (gianlucagradogna.com).** Pairs a serif ("LZ", declared `font-family: LZ, serif`) with PP Neue Montreal sans. Uses OGL (WebGL). Awwwards tags: typography, horizontal-layout, infinite-scroll, photography. Weakness: the text fetch returned only a preloader ("Loading portfolio", counter at 0) and a device prompt. Content sits behind a loader and JS render, which is bad for first paint and crawlability. — [gianlucagradogna.com (fetched)](https://gianlucagradogna.com/); [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/gianluca-gradogna-portfolio)
- **Olha Lazarieva.** The jury called it "an elegant, minimal system" and tagged it typography and gallery. Weakness: the server HTML is a 2 KB React shell, so nothing renders without JS (no SSR). The 3D tag means some WebGL. — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/olha-lazarieva); [olhalazarieva.com (fetched)](https://www.olhalazarieva.com/)
- **Rauno Freiberg (rauno.me).** Interaction-design craft rather than spectacle. Sans + JetBrains Mono, with a Georgia serif token; Next.js. It only got an Honorable Mention, but he is widely followed for interface detail (Vercel; Devouring Details). — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/portfolio-2025); [rauno.me (fetched)](https://rauno.me)
- **Grégory Lallé '24.** Awwwards tags: minimal, about-page, transitions. The source references OGL. — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/gregory-lalle-24)
- **Mat Voyce.** A type designer's own portfolio with Typography Honors. Type and animation lead, with illustration and sound. — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/mat-voyce)
- **Valentina Pastushenko (imarketina.com).** The most extreme restraint: "1 simple font, two font sizes, three colours". It only reached Honorable Mention and is built on Tilda. — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/minimalistic-portfolio)
- **Spectacle end, for contrast:** Bruno Simon (3D game world), Jesper Landberg (three.js), Pacôme Pertant (three.js + sound), Roman Jean-Elie (three.js fullscreen), Léo Parpeix (Blender/WebGL), Guillaume Colombel (three.js + sound), Merouane Bali (3D + sound). All carry 3D/WebGL tags on Awwwards or load three.js/OGL in their source. — Awwwards site pages cited above

### Inferences
- **Most relevant to donkrumpos's field-journal system (warm paper, serif-led, mono accents):** Wodniack first. He uses the same Astro/Cloudflare stack, the same serif + mono pairing logic (Editorial New + Fraktion Mono, against Fraunces/Newsreader + IBM Plex Mono), a long single-page editorial scroll, and personal-history captions. Gradogna second (serif + grotesk, photography framing). Rauno third, for interaction restraint and craft.
- No 2025–2026 individual winner I found uses a warm-paper or printmaking palette. A woodcut "hand" seems unrepresented among recent juried portfolios, so donkrumpos would be distinctive rather than derivative.
- Even the restrained winners tend to skip real case-study writing (Wodniack's cards are link-outs). An ED-facing site that leads with narrative case studies would differ from the award pool on substance, not just style.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm colors, image framing or motion quality for any site without rendering. Someone should visually check Wodniack, Gradogna, Lazarieva and Lallé.
- I did not run Lighthouse or measure performance. "Heavy" is inferred from WebGL libraries, preloaders and JS-only shells, not measured.
